# Model Card — CollectIQ models

Executed metrics are in **MODEL_RESULTS.md** (generated by the pipeline; nothing in it is
hand-typed). This card describes intended use, data, method and risks.

## Intended use
Decision support for collections teams: rank delinquent accounts, compare candidate
actions and plan resource allocation. Outputs inform a human decision; they never
authorise adverse action, legal steps or credit decisions on their own.

**Out of scope:** origination / underwriting decisions (see RiskLens), pricing, any use on
real borrowers without re-training and validation on real data.

## Models
| | Model A — roll-forward risk | Model B — payment propensity | Model C — amount given payment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Target | 90+ DPD or charge-off within 3 month-ends | any cash in the month after a decision | log(1+cash) where cash > 0 |
| Population | account-months at 1-89 DPD | account-months in a collections stage (incl. charged off ≤ 36 months) | paying opportunities |
| Candidates | Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost | Logistic Regression, XGBoost | Ridge, XGBoost regressor |
| Selection | validation ROC-AUC (LR preferred within 0.003) | same | validation MAE of expected cash (B × C) |
| Threshold | minimises expected cost, FN = 5 × FP (validation) | maximises F1 (validation); decisions use probabilities | n/a |
| Explanation | SHAP TreeExplainer (XGBoost) / exact linear contributions (LR) | linear contributions | — |

Why "1-89 DPD" for Model A: a current account can only move one bucket per month, so it
cannot reach 90+ DPD in three months. Including current accounts produced a misleading
ROC-AUC of 0.99 driven by structurally certain negatives; the population was narrowed
during development and the inflated version discarded.

Why "No action" is a decision in Model B: historical months without an event let the
model estimate cash without contact, so the prioritisation values an action by its
*incremental* cash rather than cash that would arrive anyway.

## Training data
Synthetic (`data_origin = SYNTHETIC`), seed 42, generator `synthetic-v1.1`. Chronological
splits — see MODEL_RESULTS.md for exact dates and sizes. No public data and no real
customer data were used. **The models have not been validated on real collection
outcomes.**

## Evaluation summary
Held-out test performance is moderate-to-good for A and B and modest for C (amounts are
noisy); the selected models are reasonably calibrated (calibration curves on the Model &
Monitoring page). Segment ROC-AUC is reported for product, grade and region. The
region spread is small in this data, but region is synthetic and has no effect in the
generator, so this check says little about real-world fairness.

## Fairness considerations
* No protected characteristics exist in the data. `region` is excluded from all models
  and decision rules and is used only for monitoring.
* Contact rules (restrictions, cooling-off, contact caps) are applied before any
  ranking; no model can override them.
* On real data, product, geography and income can proxy for protected attributes.
  Segment-level error and approval of contact intensity by segment should be reviewed
  before use.

## Risks and limitations
1. Synthetic data from a known mechanism — performance does not transfer to a real book.
2. Action effects are identifiable only because the simulated historical policy picked
   actions at random within stage. Real collection data is observational and confounded;
   incremental estimates would need A/B tests or causal adjustment.
3. Model C is relatively weak; expected cash is most reliable when aggregated (decile
   calibration) rather than per account.
4. Drift: `months_on_book` shows significant PSI between train and test because the
   synthetic book is seasoning. Retraining cadence should be set on real data.
5. Recommendations are associations under the training policy and must not be described
   as guaranteed recoveries.
