### Generation method

Month-by-month simulation with latent risk index, logistic hazards, stage-conditional historical collection policy and capped recoveries. Parameters in generation_parameters.json.

### Transformation history

1. generate -> data/synthetic/<dataset>/ (raw, immutable)
2. standardize names/types -> data/staging/
3. derive recovery_outcomes (event windows) and portfolio_snapshots (aggregates)
4. data-quality gate (critical failures block loading and training)
5. load SQLite in one transaction (delete + insert, idempotent)
6. feature engineering -> data/processed/

### Known limitations

* All records are synthetic; no PUBLIC observations are present.
* Collection-event outcomes are simulated and are not evidence of real-world collection effectiveness.
* Historical collection actions were assigned at random within delinquency stage; real-world action assignment is usually non-random (confounded).
* Payment timing within a month is not simulated; each month has at most one collection event per account and the month's cash is attributed to it.
* Macro stress and seasonality are stylised assumptions.
